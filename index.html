How to read the signal

OVER: the last digit is greater than your selected barrier. UNDER: the last digit is lower than your selected barrier. A digit equal to the barrier is neither outcome.

The percentages describe past ticks in the selected window. They do not reliably predict the next tick, and a high frequency is not proof of a winning trade. Test on demo and never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
